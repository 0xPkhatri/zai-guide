About this guide

Software Savings Notes is an independent project for people comparing software subscription offers. It is not operated by Z.ai.

How offers are checked

Each guide links to official sources and shows the date those sources were checked. Checkout prices, account eligibility and campaign availability can change between checks.

We distinguish invitation links from checkout coupon codes, explain important exclusions, and omit prices we cannot verify. Source checks are not purchase tests.

Corrections

If the checkout differs from a guide, use the current official terms and contact the product’s support before paying. Dates are updated only after a source review.
